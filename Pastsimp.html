Section 1: Was / Were & Could

We use was/were for the past of to be and could for past ability. NEVER use the auxiliary "did" with was, were, or could!

Affirmative: I was at home. / She could swim.
Negative: They weren't late. / He couldn't come.
Question: Were you happy? / Could she hear us?

Section 2: Action Verbs & Auxiliary "Did"

For all other verbs: use the past form (V2) in Affirmative sentences. In Negative & Interrogative sentences, use didn't / did + BASE VERB.

Affirmative: Rob bought a shirt. (No "did")
Negative: Rob didn't buy a shirt. (didn't + buy)
Question: Did Rob buy a shirt? (Did + subject + buy)
