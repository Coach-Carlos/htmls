Present Perfect vs. Past Simple Matrix

Understand connected experiences vs. completed events at specific past times.

Present Perfect

Connected to the PRESENT / Unspecified Time

"She has worked at the magazine for six months."

"I have been very busy recently."

Subject + HAVE / HAS + Past Participle (V3)
Past Simple

Finished Action / SPECIFIC Past Time

"Rob broke up with his girlfriend a few months ago."

"She started her new job last month."

Subject + Past Form (V2)
Feature Present Perfect (Have/Has + V3) Past Simple (V2)
Time Connection Unspecified past time / experience connected to present Completed time frame in the past
Signal Words recently, ever, never, for, since, so far yesterday, last year, in 2024, a few months ago
Comparison Example She has visited New York several times. She visited New York in 2024.
